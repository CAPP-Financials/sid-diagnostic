What you receive

  1. 1. Reconciliation model Your reported figure set against computed exposure, every leak path shown as arithmetic you can multiply by hand. No score without a derivation.
  2. 2. Ranked leak paths with intervals Each path carries a range, not a single number. Ranges come from the spread in your own inputs, not an asserted margin.
  3. 3. Remediation roadmap What to fix, in what order, what each fix is worth. Cheapest confirmations first.
  4. 4. Walkthrough, 60 minutes Live session with the model open. Assumptions get challenged in the room and the file is corrected on the spot.
  5. 5. The working file Yours to keep and re-run. It does not expire and is not licensed back to me.

What this does not do

  • It is not an audit and carries no assurance opinion. It cannot be filed or relied on as one.
  • It does not measure your organisation. Where you supply a band rather than a figure, the output is an estimate built on practitioner rates, and every such line says so.
  • It does not include implementation. Fixing what the diagnostic finds is separate work, quoted separately, and you may give it to someone else.
  • It is not legal, tax, or regulatory advice.
  • It needs no system access, credentials, or data export. If a question cannot be answered from figures you already hold, it stays unanswered and the range widens to show it.

How the fourteen days run

Day 0

Signature and payment clear. Intake pack sent. The clock starts when your figures arrive, not before.

Days 1 to 4

Baseline reconciliation. Where your recorded figures originate and what they omit at the point of capture.

Days 5 to 10

Leak paths computed and ranked across all three layers, each with its interval and stated assumptions.

Days 11 to 14

Roadmap drafted, walkthrough held, corrections applied, final file handed over.

What the number is worth, and when

Estimate

Banded inputs. The honest ceiling for a questionnaire. Wide ranges, stated as such.

Refined

Exact figures supplied for the lines that matter. Ranges narrow because your inputs did.

Verified

Requires source documents and a separate agreement. Not reachable inside this engagement.

This engagement delivers Estimate, and Refined on any line where you supply exact figures. A banded questionnaire cannot reach Verified, so it is not promised here.
