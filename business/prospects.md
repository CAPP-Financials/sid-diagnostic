_Built 2026-07-31. Vertical: loyalty and retail, India + SEA. Offer: $500/month Signal Integrity retainer._

The decision ledger calls this "the largest single risk in the plan and the only one that is not technical."
The goal is **10 named people**, not 500 rows. A $500/month retainer sold by one person does not need volume,
it needs the right ten.

## Why this vertical, not banking or enterprise-AI

The SID engine ships three domains. Only one of them is backed by a track record you can defend on a call.

| Domain | Your provable track record | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Loyalty / retail | EasyRewardz, 7 SEA markets, 5,300+ stores, 10–12M customers, $8M incremental revenue, 8.2x ROI, 6% churn reduction → $2.3M | **Lead with this** |
| Banking / fraud | None | Engine works, story does not. Do not cold-pitch it. |
| Enterprise AI | None named | Trendiest, weakest proof. Inbound only. |

Cold outreach in a vertical where you have no client proof is the exact failure of the trash-review playbook:
it borrowed real numbers from loyalty work and re-attributed them to a fictional migration-corruption story.
Lead with loyalty and every number in the email is one you actually produced.

## Ideal customer profile

**Must have all four:**

1. **A running loyalty program with real reward liability.** No program means no reward-value figures to
   reconcile, and the diagnostic has nothing to compute against.
2. **Revenue roughly $30M–$800M** (₹250 cr – ₹6,500 cr). Large enough that a 0.1% leak is worth more than
   the retainer by two orders of magnitude. Small enough that the person who owns the number will take a
   meeting.
3. **A named owner of the loyalty P&L.** If nobody owns it, nobody buys a diagnostic of it.
4. **Monthly figures actually exist and can be sent.** The retainer's cycle dies if intake does not arrive.
   Ask this in discovery, not after signing.

**Disqualify immediately:**

- **Enterprise conglomerates** (Tata, Reliance, Shell, Sephora, Domino's and the rest of the Capillary
  enterprise roster). Procurement will not process a $500 invoice from an individual with no entity. The
  cost of their vendor-onboarding exceeds the contract value. Revisit once Capp Financial Services exists.
- **Pre-loyalty startups.** Nothing to reconcile.
- **Anyone who needs you to sign an MSA with insurance and indemnity clauses.** You have no entity and no
  professional indemnity cover. Walk.
- **Current or recent EasyRewardz clients** — see the conflict note below.

## Sourcing method, repeatable

This is the part to run monthly, not a one-time list build.

**1. Vendor case-study mining (highest yield).**
Loyalty platform vendors publish their client rosters as marketing. That roster *is* the list of companies
running loyalty at scale. Capillary alone claims 400+ enterprises across apparel, F&B and QSR, supermarkets,
fuel retail, and pharmacy in India, the Middle East, and SEA. Work the case-study pages of Capillary, Xeno,
Zinrelo, Nector, and RewardPort. Filter out the enterprise names, keep the mid-market.

**2. Job-posting signal.**
Companies hiring a Loyalty Manager, CRM Analyst, or Customer Data Lead are companies that just admitted the
function needs attention. The posting also names the reporting line, which tells you who to approach.

**3. Platform-change announcements.**
Vendor press releases announcing a new client are a dated migration signal. That is the 3-point signal above,
served to you.

**4. App-store review mining.**
Search a brand's app reviews for "points not credited", "reward missing", "balance wrong". Customer-visible
points discrepancies are downstream evidence of exactly the reconciliation failure SID computes. This is the
single most useful qualifier available for free, and it gives you a concrete opening line.

## Who to approach, by title

In priority order. One person per company, not three.

1. **Head of Loyalty / Loyalty Programme Manager** — owns the number the diagnostic reconciles
2. **Head of CRM / Customer Data** — owns the systems the figures come from
3. **VP / Head of Retail Finance or FP&A** — owns whether the liability on the books is right
4. **Founder or CEO** — only at the smaller end, where they still own the P&L personally

Skip the CTO. The problem is a reporting and finance problem wearing a data costume, and the CTO will
route it to a backlog.

Find the actual person on LinkedIn. Do not buy a scraped contact list: deliverability on purchased data is
poor, and it puts your only sending domain at risk before you have a single client.

## Conflict of interest — read before contacting anyone

**Do not approach EasyRewardz clients without checking your exit paperwork first.**

You worked there Jun 2022 – Aug 2024. Indian courts generally do not enforce post-employment non-compete
clauses, but **non-solicitation clauses covering clients are a different question** and are treated more
seriously, particularly where confidential client information was involved. Your strongest proof points
(the 7-market, 5,300-store engagement) came from that client relationship.

Two separate risks:
1. **Contractual** — a non-solicit clause in your employment agreement may bar approaching their client list.
2. **Confidentiality** — the $8M and 6% churn figures may be covered by a client NDA that survives your
   employment, regardless of what your own contract says.

Action before any outreach goes out: re-read the EasyRewardz employment agreement and any client-specific
NDA. If the numbers are restricted, the outreach copy has to be rewritten to describe the *method* and the
*scale* of the engagement without the specific client figures. That is a solvable copy problem, but only if
you find out now instead of after a letter arrives.

I have not seen these documents and cannot advise on them. This is a flag, not a legal opinion.
